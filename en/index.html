The situation

Always connected. What if we met up more?

Between classes, screens and days that all look alike, it isn't always easy to meet new people, find activities that suit us or take time for ourselves.

That's the idea VIBES was born from.

Why VIBES exists

Behind the screen, four things that add up.

It's not a lack of willpower. It's what we see around us, on campus.

  1. 1

    Attraction

    Scrolling is designed to hold you. Staying says nothing about your willpower.

  2. 2

    The void

    When you put the phone down, you don't always know what to do that's as good, or with whom.

  3. 3

    The silence

    We often know what we need. What's missing is a place to admit it without being judged.

  4. 4

    The invisible

    Well-being tools exist, but many students don't know about them, or don't see themselves in them.

What adults often misunderstand

They see a lack of willpower. We mostly observe a lack of alternatives and of people to turn to: when screens isolate, it also becomes harder to find a real listening ear, for example in cases of harassment or a health problem. That is why VIBES plans trusted contacts and free helplines, one tap away.

Questionnaire of 99 students (ENSGMM, ENSTP, ENSGEP and others): 73% spend 4 hours or more a day on their phone, 48% find that time too long, 28% often lack company. Convenience sample, self-reported: it does not represent all students.

The approach

Three gestures. Another way to live campus life.

  • Discover

    Meet young people who share your passions. Explore activities, discover clubs and find chances to take the first step.

  • Equip yourself

    Take a moment for yourself, put words on your mood and discover simple tips to take better care of yourself.

  • Anchor

    Turn small intentions into habits. Try new activities, take on small Étincelles (Sparks) and move at your own pace.

Five dimensions of well-being: social connection · physical well-being · emotional balance · mental well-being · food.

No need to change everything. Sometimes one small action is enough to get started.

What sets us apart

We're not trying to keep you in the app.

Many apps are measured by the time you spend in them. VIBES is measured by the time you spend in it less.

Our central indicator: lived hours. These are the real hours of matches, walks, choir rehearsals or creating that VIBES helped trigger.

Time spent in the app is tracked as a warning signal, not as a goal.

Indicator still being defined. No result is claimed at this stage.

The Crew

The first step is easier when you're not alone.

A match, a walk, a music session or a new activity: sometimes all it takes is finding a few people to share the moment with.

  1. 1You spot an activity that speaks to you.
  2. 2You join a small group to get organised.
  3. 3You meet up in person, for real.

Planned: a chat reserved for people signed up to the activity, with no private messages. The Crew is for taking part, not for replacing real life.

Our commitments

Your well-being. Your pace. Your choices.

A clear limit. VIBES is a prevention and well-being initiative. It is not a care service, a diagnostic tool, or medical or psychological advice.

  1. Respect for your privacy

    What you share in your personal space stays private, according to the rules set by VIBES.

  2. Freedom to choose

    You choose which activities you want to join and what you want to share.

  3. No pressure

    No competition over your well-being, no guilt when you take a break.

  4. Supervised activities

    Events will be checked before publication, and organisers will have to follow the safety rules set by the project.

Made for the daily life of students in Benin

Nearly one student in five has no regular internet access, and 71% would spend 1,000 FCFA or less on an activity: we think about it from the start. Prévu dans la conception

  • A lightweight app. A web app, designed for small data plans and older phones.
  • Know before you go. Real cost, distance and duration shown before you commit.
  • Free first. Free activities highlighted, so that budget doesn't get in the way of wanting to go.
  • In our own words. French first, with tips written by young Beninese people. Local languages are being studied.
  • For beginners. Activities open to all levels, with accessibility information.

When it is too much, you are not alone.

Need help?

A "Need help?" access will be visible on every screen. In one tap, it points you to the right specialised service, depending on what you need.

  • Harassment and violence

    Listening and reporting services, to talk about it and know what to do.

    National Institute for Women (INF): 114

  • Physical health

    Health centres and professionals to turn to.

    Health: 136 · Medical emergency (SAMU): 112

  • Mental health

    Professionals in listening and psychological support.

    Contacts being verified

  • A trusted person

    A relative, a teacher or a classmate: you choose who to tell.

Numbers taken from the official short-number list published by ARCEP Benin. We verify each service with its managers before showing it in the app. The 138 line is for children who are victims of violence.

VIBES points you to help; it does not replace a professional. It is not a care service.

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers.

Is VIBES available yet?

Not yet. The project is being designed: the mockups exist, but the app can't be downloaded yet.

Is VIBES a medical service?

No. It's a prevention and well-being initiative. VIBES makes no diagnosis, gives no score, and points you to a trusted person or service if it's too much.

Does what I write stay private?

What you write in My Safe Place is private, according to the rules set by VIBES. Answers about how you feel are optional.

Is VIBES a social network or a dating app?

No. No public feed, no likes, no rankings. Activities are public and are lived in small groups.
